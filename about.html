ABOUT

A home-run
micro-cafe.

The Corner Cuppa is a small home-run coffee and matcha concept serving the Boon Lay/Jurong neighbourhood.

The identity is intentionally intimate: a counter, a focused menu and drinks that feel considered without losing the ease of a neighbourhood stop.

Exact residential information is kept limited here. The public-facing address used for this site is the business's published location.
