## Site map
Home / Menu / Coffee Lab / Order / Seasonal Bakes / About / Pickup / FAQ / Contact.

## Experience
Hero: Vietnamese coffee or signature matcha visual. CTA: Order Pickup. Menu should clearly separate Coffee / Matcha / Non-caffeine / Weekend Bakes. Product cards include availability and “order through current platform”.

## Visual
Jurong-neighbourhood warmth with premium editorial minimalism: cream, cocoa, deep coffee, muted berry accent. Strong typography and product close-ups.

## Motion/VFX
Liquid coffee ripple shader in hero; scroll-based blur/translate/stagger; drink-card 3D hover; ingredient orbit on desktop; image masks; subtle grain; page transition with rounded clip-path. Never sacrifice readability or performance.

## Functional
Current hours/status, sold-out state, seasonal archive, order-platform deep link, Instagram, map/area pickup, enquiry form, accessibility, SEO. Keep residential privacy intact.
